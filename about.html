Charm

A pocket app for the small things: money, tasks, habits, notes and a focus timer, with a friendly little assistant. It works offline and keeps your data on your phone.

Money

Log expenses in a few taps and see your week, month and where it went, in your own currency.

Tasks

Type "pay rent every month" or "call mum tomorrow at 5pm" and the date and repeat are filled in.

Habits

Daily check-ins with streaks and gentle reminders.

Focus

A timer that keeps counting when your phone sleeps.

Chat

Say "spent 15 on lunch" and it's logged. Ask money questions and get answers from a built-in guide.

Data usage

On Android, see how much mobile data you've used and get a warning before your plan runs out.

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your data is saved on the phone as an encrypted vault that only your passcode opens.
  • Lock the app with your passcode, fingerprint or face.
  • Reinstall or switch phones, enter your passcode, and everything comes back.
  • Optional features, such as reading payment alerts, are off until you turn them on, and each one tells you exactly what it reads first.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

Made to be easy to use

Charm has a dark mode that follows your phone, works with large text, supports screen readers, and can reduce animations.
